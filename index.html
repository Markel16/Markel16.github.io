Hunter · Player · Hunted

Unreal Engine 5.6.1 Game AI C++

Individual AI for Video Games project: a scenario with three entities — the player, a Hunter that chases and patrols, and a Hunted that flees and alerts the Hunter — built from a Blank Project and implemented mostly in C++.

  • Two dedicated Behavior Trees (BT_Chaser and BT_Prey) driven by a C++ AIController
  • AI Perception with hearing as the dominant sense (noise detection) and sight as secondary
  • EQS (Environment Query System) to calculate smart flee positions for the Hunted
  • Stealth system: reduced speed avoids generating noise and being detected
  • Dynamic audio: tension music that rises as the Hunter gets closer
